Dashbox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Dashbox is a record-keeping app for vehicle receipts and mileage. Your data stays on your device. Dashbox has no account and no server, and it never uploads your records.

What's stored, and where

Vehicles, fuel/maintenance/parking receipts, receipt photos, trips, saved places, and odometer readings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use iCloud or iTunes/Finder backups, this data is included in those backups, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Location

When you track a drive, the app uses your location to measure distance — only while tracking, and only with the permission you grant in iOS. Your location is used solely to calculate distance and is not shared with anyone.

Receipt scanning

Photos you attach are read on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. The images are not sent anywhere.

Maps

When you calculate a trip distance between two addresses, those addresses are sent to Apple Maps to look up the route. When you tap a Google Maps directions link, the destination opens in Google Maps, which is operated by Google under its own privacy policy.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
